Privacy Policy for King Me
Effective date: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how King Me (the “Game”) accesses, uses, stores, and discloses information. It applies to the Android version of King Me and other versions of the Game that link to this policy.

Information stored on your device
King Me stores game progress and preferences locally on your device. This may include saved games, unlocked cards, scores and statistics, tutorial progress, language and audio settings, and leaderboard preferences. This information is used to save your progress and operate the Game. It is not sent to the developer unless it is included in the leaderboard information described below.

You can remove locally stored information by clearing the Game’s app data or uninstalling it. Depending on your Android and Google account settings, Android may back up some app data through your device’s backup service.

Global leaderboard information
King Me offers an optional global leaderboard. If you use the leaderboard, the Game processes and stores:

a display name chosen by you;
a randomly generated player identifier created on your device;
your qualifying level score;
the active play time associated with that score; and
the date and time the leaderboard record was updated.
Your display name, score, and associated play time may be visible to other players. Do not use your real name or include an email address, phone number, location, or other personal information in your display name. King Me does not require a user account or password.

Technical information processed by service providers
The leaderboard uses Google Firebase Realtime Database. When the Game connects to Firebase, Google may process technical information such as an IP address and user-agent information to provide, secure, and maintain the service. Firebase states that Realtime Database generally retains IP addresses and user-agent information for a few days unless configured for longer retention.

The Game also requests Firebase software files and font files hosted by Google. Those requests may provide Google with ordinary connection data, such as an IP address, browser or device information, and the date and time of the request. Google handles this information under its own privacy terms.

How information is used
Information is used only to:

operate the Game and save local progress;
display, rank, and update leaderboard entries;
maintain leaderboard integrity and prevent abuse;
troubleshoot technical problems; and
comply with legal obligations and enforce applicable policies.
Advertising, sale, and tracking
King Me does not contain third-party advertising. The developer does not sell personal information, use leaderboard information for targeted advertising, or use the Game to track users across unrelated apps or websites.

Service providers and disclosure
Leaderboard and related technical information is processed by Google as a service provider through Firebase and Google-hosted resources. Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect users, investigate fraud or abuse, or protect the rights and security of the Game and its services. Other than the public leaderboard fields described above, the developer does not intentionally make player information public.

Learn more at Firebase Privacy and Security and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion
Local game information remains on your device until it is overwritten, cleared, or the Game is uninstalled. Leaderboard entries may be retained while the leaderboard operates so that personal-best scores and rankings can be maintained. A newer qualifying score may replace an earlier score.

You may request deletion of your leaderboard entry by using the contact information below. Include the display name shown on the leaderboard and enough information to identify the correct entry. Additional information may be requested when reasonably necessary to prevent deletion of another player’s entry. Verified requests will be handled within a reasonable period, subject to legal, security, and backup-retention requirements.

Children’s privacy
King Me is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed specifically to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through the leaderboard, contact the developer so it can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate.

Security
Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information. Leaderboard data is transmitted to Firebase using HTTPS encryption. However, no storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

International processing
Google and Firebase may process information on servers located outside your state, province, or country. Data-protection laws in those locations may differ from those where you live.

Changes to this policy
This policy may be updated when the Game or its information practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when an updated policy is published.
